How Pioneer membership works

  • Evaluation: 14 days, starting when you first sign in. No charge.
  • After you buy in: 60 additional days of beta access on the tier you bought (GROW or FLOW). This is extra beta access — not two paid months.
  • After those 60 days: you move to free MODE until public launch. MODE includes tapping and logging; Flashy™ voice time is limited, and we'll announce the amounts before launch.
  • At public launch: your monthly subscription begins at your Pioneer rate. Your Pioneer rate is available for 24 calendar months from public launch. It is not a contract — you can cancel anytime, with no minimum term. After the 24 months, public pricing applies, and we'll give you 30 days' notice first.
  • If you don't buy in: no charge. Your account moves to free MODE and you can keep using it. You can still join Pioneer pricing later, before the 24-month window ends, at a re-entry rate of $7.99/mo (GROW) or $16.99/mo (FLOW), starting at public launch.
  • If you cancel later: your account moves to free MODE and your data is kept. If you bought in, you can return at your own Pioneer rate until the 24-month window ends.
  • If you delete your account: everything is permanently erased. There is no MODE access and no Pioneer rate afterward.

Pause instead of cancelling

After public launch, you can pause your paid subscription for 30 or 60 days, up to twice per 12 months. While paused, you're on free MODE, you aren't charged, and your data is kept. When the pause ends, your subscription restarts and your card is charged unless you cancel first — we'll email you 7 days before. Pausing does not extend your 24-month Pioneer window. Prepaid annual plans can't be paused. If a charge fails, we retry for about 7 days; if it still can't be completed, your subscription ends and your account moves to free MODE.

Refund & cancellation policy

You can cancel anytime — your access continues through the end of your current billing period, and we don't do partial-month refunds for time already used. Pioneer Beta Members get a free two-week evaluation before any buy-in is charged; once that buy-in is completed, it's final and non-refundable, though you can still pause or cancel your subscription afterward (see the full terms on the Beta Tester page). If pricing ever changes, we'll always notify you at least 30 days in advance, and you're guaranteed to keep your current rate for the rest of that billing cycle plus one additional full month before any new price applies.
