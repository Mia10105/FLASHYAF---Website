Pioneer Beta Member pricing

Pioneer Beta Members get a different path: a free two-week evaluation, then an optional one-time buy-in that gives you 60 more days of beta access, which depends on taking part in testing, and a founding-member monthly rate for 24 months from public launch — well below public pricing. You can buy in during Round 1 (days 1–14) or, at a slightly higher price, in a final Round 2 (days 15–28).

How Pioneer membership works

  • Days 1–14, evaluation: a free 14-day evaluation with full FLOW features, starting when your beta account is created. No charge. Round 1 buy-in is available during this time at the lower price.
  • Days 15–28, final chance: if you haven't bought in, your account moves to free MODE and you get one final chance to buy in at the Round 2 price. Round 2 is slightly higher than Round 1.
  • After you buy in (either round): 60 additional days of beta access (a Pioneer benefit that depends on taking part in testing). This is extra beta access — not two paid months. Buying in does not start any monthly billing before public launch.
  • After those 60 days: our current plan is that you move to free MODE until public launch. MODE includes tapping and logging; Flashy™ voice time is limited, and we'll announce the amounts before launch.
  • At public launch: your monthly subscription begins at your Pioneer rate. Your Pioneer rate is available for 24 calendar months from public launch. It is not a contract — you can cancel anytime, with no minimum term. After the 24 months, public pricing applies, and we'll give you 30 days' notice first.
  • If you don't buy in by the end of day 28: no charge. Your beta access ends until public launch. Your account and data are held until 6 months after public launch, so you can pick up where you left off without creating a new account. If you haven't returned by then, the held account and data are removed and you would need to create a new account. You can delete your account and data at any time.
  • If you cancel later: at public launch, if you cancel your paid subscription, your account is planned to move to free MODE and your data is kept. If you bought in, you can return before your original 24-month window ends at your original Pioneer monthly rate ($5.99/mo GROW or $14.99/mo FLOW). You won't pay another buy-in, and your original 24-month clock continues — it doesn't restart. After the window ends, the special Pioneer and return pricing ends and public pricing applies.
  • If you delete your account: deleting is separate from cancelling. Deletion is intended to permanently erase your account and personal app history, with no MODE access and no Pioneer rate afterward. We will confirm the final details before public launch.

Refund & cancellation policy

At public launch, you will be able to cancel anytime, and your access is planned to continue through the end of your current billing period. We don't do partial-month refunds for time already used. Pioneer Beta Members get a free two-week evaluation before any buy-in is charged; once that buy-in is completed, it's final and non-refundable, though you can still cancel your subscription afterward. If pricing ever changes, we'll always notify you at least 30 days in advance, and you're guaranteed to keep your current rate for the rest of that billing cycle plus one additional full month before any new price applies.
