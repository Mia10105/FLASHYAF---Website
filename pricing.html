Pioneer Beta Member pricing

Pioneer Beta members get a different path: a free two-week evaluation, then an optional one-time buy-in that gives you 60 more days of beta access on your tier and a founding-member monthly rate for 24 months from public launch — well below public pricing. The buy-in is limited to 100 spots (60 GROW, 40 FLOW).

How Pioneer membership works

  • Evaluation: 14 days, starting when you first sign in. No charge.
  • After you buy in: 60 additional days of beta access on the tier you bought (GROW or FLOW). This is extra beta access — not two paid months.
  • After those 60 days: you move to free MODE until public launch. MODE includes tapping and logging; Flashy™ voice time is limited, and we'll announce the amounts before launch.
  • At public launch: your monthly subscription begins at your Pioneer rate. Your Pioneer rate is available for 24 calendar months from public launch. It is not a contract — you can cancel anytime, with no minimum term. After the 24 months, public pricing applies, and we'll give you 30 days' notice first.
  • If you don't buy in: no charge. Your account moves to free MODE and you can keep using it. Because you took part in the Pioneer Beta, you can return during the 24-month window at the Pioneer Beta Return Rate of $7.99/mo (GROW) or $16.99/mo (FLOW). No buy-in is required. This isn't the lowest Pioneer rate, which is for members who complete the buy-in.
  • If you cancel later: your account moves to free MODE and your data is kept. If you bought in, you can return before your original 24-month window ends at your original Pioneer monthly rate ($5.99/mo GROW or $14.99/mo FLOW). You won't pay another buy-in, and your original 24-month clock continues — it doesn't restart. After the window ends, the special Pioneer and return pricing ends and public pricing applies.
  • If you delete your account: everything is permanently erased. There is no MODE access and no Pioneer rate afterward.

Pioneer Beta applications are being updated and will reopen soon. Join the waitlist to be notified.

Refund & cancellation policy

You can cancel anytime — your access continues through the end of your current billing period, and we don't do partial-month refunds for time already used. Pioneer Beta Members get a free two-week evaluation before any buy-in is charged; once that buy-in is completed, it's final and non-refundable, though you can still cancel your subscription afterward. If pricing ever changes, we'll always notify you at least 30 days in advance, and you're guaranteed to keep your current rate for the rest of that billing cycle plus one additional full month before any new price applies.
