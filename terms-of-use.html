Pioneer Beta Evaluation, Buy-In, Access, and Retention

The Pioneer Beta begins with a 14-calendar-day evaluation with full FLOW features. During Days 1–14, an eligible participant may complete a one-time Round 1 Pioneer buy-in of $14.99 for GROW or $39.99 for FLOW. A participant who does not buy in by the end of Day 14 moves to MODE Free for Days 15–28 and has one final opportunity to complete a one-time Round 2 Pioneer buy-in of $19.99 for GROW or $44.99 for FLOW. If no buy-in is completed by the end of Day 28, beta access ends until public launch. A completed Pioneer buy-in provides 60 additional beta calendar days at the selected Pioneer tier, after which access moves to MODE Free until public launch. Pioneer recurring monthly billing does not begin before public launch. For Pioneer Beta participants who do not have a paid subscription at public launch, the account and associated app data may be held until 6 months after public launch so the participant can return to the existing account. If the participant has not come back to use the app by the end of that period, the held account and associated app data will be removed. A participant may request account deletion earlier at any time; such requests are handled under the separate account-deletion process described in the Privacy Policy.
